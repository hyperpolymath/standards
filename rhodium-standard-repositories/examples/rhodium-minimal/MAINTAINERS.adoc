=== 🎯 Becoming a Maintainer

==== Path from Community to Trusted Contributor

[arabic]
. *Start Contributing*: Submit high-quality MRs
. *Build Trust*: Consistent adherence to standards
. *Demonstrate Expertise*: Deep understanding of codebase
. *Apply*: Express interest via issue
. *Review*: Existing maintainers evaluate
. *Approval*: Added to trusted contributor list

==== Path from Trusted Contributor to Maintainer

This is rare and requires: - Significant architectural contributions -
Long-term commitment demonstrated - Community support - Maintainer
consensus
