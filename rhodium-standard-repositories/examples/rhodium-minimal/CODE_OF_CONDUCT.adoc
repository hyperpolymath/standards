== Code of Conduct

*Rhodium Standard Repository (RSR) - Community Standards*

'''''

=== 🤝 Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make
participation in our project and community a *harassment-free experience
for everyone*, regardless of:

* Age
* Body size
* Visible or invisible disability
* Ethnicity
* Sex characteristics
* Gender identity and expression
* Level of experience
* Education
* Socio-economic status
* Nationality
* Personal appearance
* Race
* Religion
* Sexual identity and orientation
* Technical background

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an *open,
welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and emotionally safe community*.

'''''

=== ✅ Our Standards

==== Behaviour We Encourage

* ✅ *Using welcoming and inclusive language*
* ✅ *Being respectful of differing viewpoints* and experiences
* ✅ *Gracefully accepting constructive criticism*
* ✅ *Focusing on what is best for the community*
* ✅ *Showing empathy towards other community members*
* ✅ *Assuming good intentions* (Hanlon’s Razor: "`Never attribute to
malice that which is adequately explained by misunderstanding`")
* ✅ *Celebrating diverse technical approaches* (polyglot by design)
* ✅ *Acknowledging mistakes* and learning from them
* ✅ *Prioritising emotional safety* alongside technical excellence

==== Behaviour We Discourage

* ❌ *Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments*, and personal or
political attacks
* ❌ *Public or private harassment*
* ❌ *Publishing others’ private information* (doxxing) without explicit
permission
* ❌ *Sexual language or imagery*, and unwelcome sexual attention or
advances
* ❌ *Gatekeeping* ("`you’re not a real programmer if…`")
* ❌ *Assuming incompetence* based on identity or background
* ❌ *Dismissing concerns* about inclusivity or safety
* ❌ *Other conduct* which could reasonably be considered inappropriate
in a professional setting

'''''

=== 🛡️ Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

[arabic]
. *Clarifying* the standards of acceptable behaviour
. *Taking appropriate and fair corrective action* in response to
behaviour deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful
. *Removing, editing, or rejecting* comments, commits, code, wiki edits,
issues, and other contributions not aligned with this Code of Conduct
. *Communicating reasons* for moderation decisions when appropriate

'''''

=== 🌍 Scope

This Code of Conduct applies:

* ✅ *Within project spaces* (GitLab repository, issues, merge requests,
wiki)
* ✅ *In public spaces* when representing the project (conferences,
social media, email)
* ✅ *In community spaces* (chat rooms, forums, mailing lists)

Representation includes: - Using an official project email address -
Posting via official social media accounts - Acting as an appointed
representative at events

'''''

=== 🚨 Enforcement

==== Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may
be reported to:

*Contact*: See `+.well-known/security.txt+` for current contact
information

*What to Include*: 1. Your contact information 2. Names (real,
usernames, pseudonyms) of individuals involved 3. Description of the
incident 4. Links to public evidence (if applicable) 5. Any additional
context

*Confidentiality*: All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
*confidentially*.

==== Response Timeline

[cols=",",options="header",]
|===
|Timeline |Action
|*24 hours* |Acknowledgement of report
|*72 hours* |Preliminary assessment
|*7 days* |Determination and action (if needed)
|===

==== Enforcement Guidelines

We follow these guidelines in determining consequences:

===== 1. Correction (Minor Issue)

*Community Impact*: Inappropriate language or unprofessional behaviour.

*Consequence*: Private written warning, clarification of why the
behaviour was inappropriate, and request for apology.

===== 2. Warning (Moderate Issue)

*Community Impact*: Violation through a single incident or series of
actions.

*Consequence*: Warning with consequences for continued behaviour. No
interaction with the people involved for a specified period. Violation
may result in temporary or permanent ban.

===== 3. Temporary Ban (Serious Issue)

*Community Impact*: Serious violation of community standards, including
sustained inappropriate behaviour.

*Consequence*: Temporary ban from all project interaction and public
communication for a specified period. No public or private interaction
with the people involved during this period.

===== 4. Permanent Ban (Severe Issue)

*Community Impact*: Pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression
toward individuals or groups.

*Consequence*: Permanent ban from all public project interaction.

'''''

=== 🔄 Reversibility & Redemption

In keeping with RSR principles of *reversibility and emotional safety*:

* *Mistakes are learning opportunities*: We assume good faith and
provide chances to correct behaviour
* *Bans are reversible*: After demonstrating changed behaviour and
understanding, individuals may appeal
* *Growth is valued*: We recognise that people can change and improve

*Appeal Process*: 1. Wait a minimum cooling-off period (30 days for
temporary bans) 2. Submit appeal via email (see
`+.well-known/security.txt+`) 3. Demonstrate understanding of what went
wrong 4. Outline steps taken to prevent recurrence 5. Community review
of appeal

'''''

=== 🎯 RSR-Specific Values

This Code of Conduct is grounded in *CCCP principles*:

==== Emotional Temperature

* *Reduce anxiety*: No shame in mistakes, safe experimentation
* *Increase safety*: Harassment-free, inclusive environment
* *Reversibility*: Second chances, redemption paths

==== Social Temperature

* *Warm community*: Welcoming, supportive, empathetic
* *Cool-headed decisions*: Evidence-based, not reactionary
* *Graduated trust*: TPCF framework (Perimeter 3 → 2 → 1)

==== Political Temperature

* *Anti-monopoly*: Diverse tooling, no vendor lock-in
* *Autonomy*: Offline-first, no forced connectivity
* *Mutual aid*: Helping each other succeed
