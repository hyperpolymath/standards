[.lead]
The owner found the template with what looked like only an Adjustfile in its
contractiles folder, A2ML still being written in new shapes, deeds and K9 files
in several places, a deprecated directory name still in use, and a standards
tree whose folders do not explain themselves. This document measures all of
that with the canonical tools, names the root causes, reviews the 2026-10-19
Copilot conversation on assurance envelopes, exnovation and Panoply, and lays
out a phased plan: sort out, differentiate, design and develop, then deploy.

Every count below was produced by running a validator, not by reading a status
document. The commands are in <<method>>. Where a claim could not be checked
in this session it says so.

== Verdict in one page

*The grammars are mostly sound; the system around them is not.* `deed.abnf`
v1.2.0, `K9-CONTRACT-SPEC` v1.0.0 and its Nickel contract, and the
`coordination.k9` ABNF v1.2.0 are careful, versioned and tested documents. The
deed linter passes its 67 tests; the K9 validator passes its self-test and all
27 fixtures with Nickel installed. The defects are around them:

. *One word names several things.* `k9` names three artefacts, `must` three,
  `Mustfile` two, `manifest` four, and `adjust`, `dust` and `bust` each carry two
  or three incompatible definitions in live documents (<<collisions>>). An agent
  that reads two of those documents in one session will merge them. This is the
  single largest cause of "LLMs keep missing it".
. *Retirement was done by text substitution.* `hyperpolymath/panoply` holds 134
  `.deed` files; one passes the grammar. The rest are A2ML bodies, INI bodies
  and per-directory manifests renamed to `.deed`. The same substitution was
  applied to its CI gate, which now fails on *any* `.deed` file, its own valid
  deed included (<<panoply>>). This is the "A2ML still being created in various
  forms" the owner saw.
. *Load-bearing inputs were retired without replacements.* The Must gate reads
  `Mustfile.a2ml`; a weekly CI job rewrites `Debtfile.a2ml`; Hypatia loads its
  standards rules from `hypatia-rules/*.a2ml`; the contractile CLI reads only
  `*file.a2ml`. "Never add a fact to an `.a2ml`" and "this gate needs the
  `.a2ml`" are both in force.
. *Three of the four places agents look first are stale.* The deed spec
  document says "DRAFT … Nothing in this document is deployed" while the
  grammar it describes is normative and gated. The Chora spec links to files
  that do not exist. `standards/coordination.k9` still tells agents A2ML is the
  metadata format and that Deno is banned. The contractiles repository's README
  says contractiles are "serialised in a2ml".
. *No artefact has a single home.* Repo facts are split between the deed and
  `coordination.k9`; the contractile layout exists in three incompatible forms in
  `standards` alone; templates are copied from four different sources.
. *Versions do not line up.* A deed declares `:schema-version "1.0.0"` against a
  grammar at v1.2.0 described by a spec document at v0.2.3. Only the K9 contract
  has a written versioning rule (contract version vs schema major).

*What is already moving.* Two open template PRs from today bring all 13
remaining K9 records to contract v1.0.0 and replace the template's private
validators with the canonical reusable lanes
(hyperpolymath/rsr-template-repo#247, hyperpolymath/rsr-template-repo#248).
This plan builds on them and does not redo them.

*What the Copilot material is worth.* Its assurance envelope (claims,
assumptions, scope, evidence, boundaries, failure modes) is a good shape, and
the estate already has most of it in `0-canon/constitution/ASSURANCE-CONSTITUTION.adoc`
and in Panoply's own charter. Its numeric layer should not be adopted as
written: the arithmetic checks out, but the inputs are invented, the outcome
flips under small changes, and four of its formulas have defects
(<<copilot>>). Its historical narrative, that AffineScript's theory was
"exnovated" and Panoply replaced it, contradicts Panoply's README, which says
Panoply "is not a rejection of AffineScript".

*First step.* Stop the bleeding before any redesign: a gate that rejects a
`.deed` file that does not lint, in every repo, and the collision register in
the canon. Then the rulings in <<decisions>>, then the deed-era runner, then the
wave.

=== Why the template "only has an Adjustfile"

It has no file named after any verb. The template folded each verb's
declaration into the `declaration` field of `<verb>/<verb>.k9.ncl`, next to its
runner `<verb>.ncl`: two files per verb, no A2ML. That is the right direction.
But the README in the same directory still tells a new repo to "update the
Mustfile", "point the Trustfile at real keys" and so on, so a reader looks for
files that were deliberately removed. Two further gaps make it look incomplete:

* the deed has no `(contractiles …)` clause, which D312 requires; and
* the deed's `:contractiles_required` lists three of the six verbs
  (`must`, `trust`, `intend`).

The canon's own `.machine_readable/contractiles/` still ships the older
four-file A2ML form, and its `1-formats/contractiles/` ships a third. A reader
comparing the template with the canon sees three answers and no ruling.

== Root cause 2: retirement by substitution

`hyperpolymath/panoply` is the clearest specimen. Its layout, including
`machine-readable-design/canonical-directory-structure/`, matches the contractiles
repository rather than the current template, so that is its likely source
(inferred from the layout; its history was not read):

* 134 `.deed` files, of which 133 fail the grammar. Their first lines are `#`
  comments, `@abstract:` blocks, INI sections, YAML. They are A2ML renamed.
* 95 of them are per-directory `0.N-AI-MANIFEST.deed` files, a family that has no
  deed form at all.
* The verb directories hold `0.1-AI-MANIFEST.deed` and a README; the verb
  declarations sit flat in `contractiles/` as `Mustfile.deed` and friends.
* Its `dogfood-gate.yml` deed job was edited by the same substitution:
  `find … \( -name '*.a2ml' -o -name '*.deed' \) … exit 1` under the message
  "Deprecated .a2ml files present". The gate now fails on every `.deed`,
  including the one valid chora deed.
* `CLAUDE.md` tells agents to read `0-AI-MANIFEST.deed` first. That file fails
  the grammar on line 1.

The mapping-spec rule "no renames, only translations"
(`1-formats/deed/mappings/ai-manifest-decision.adoc` §4) predicted exactly this
class and assigned the defence to "the whole-tree deed-lint CI gate". That gate
exists (`deed-conformance.yml`), but until today no consumer called it. The
template's private validators, which every minted repo copied, ran the
`deed-ecosystem` and `k9-ecosystem` actions at `@main`, and those passed the
files.

How many repos look like panoply is not known from this session; the estate
census is experiment X1.

== Root cause 6: version numbers without a rule

[cols="2,1,1,1",options="header"]
|===
|Format |Grammar |Spec document |Instance field

|deed |`deed.abnf` v1.2.0 |v0.2.3 |`:schema-version "1.0.0"`
|K9 component |contract v1.0.0 |v1.0.0 |`pedigree.schema_version` (major must match)
|coordination.k9 |ABNF v1.2.0 |v1.2.0 |`metadata.schema_version: 1.2.0`
|contractile |none |v1.2.1 |a `pedigree` from `_base.ncl`
|===

K9 is the model: it separates the contract's version from the schema major a
component must carry, and states what a reader does with another major (refuse)
and a lower minor (accept). The deed has no such rule, so `:schema-version
"1.0.0"` cannot be read as anything.

== Root cause 7: no single home per question

* *Facts in two places.* `standards/coordination.k9` carries `project`,
  `ecosystem` and `architecture`; the deed carries `(identity)`, `(ecosystem)`,
  `(meta)` and `(agentic)`. The Chora spec says the deed is "epistemic, not
  operational (operational rules belong in `coordination.k9`)", but nothing
  enforces it.
* *Templates from four sources.* `rsr-template-repo`, the contractiles
  repository's `machine-readable-design/canonical-directory-structure/`,
  `standards/1-formats/contractiles/` and `standards/.machine_readable/contractiles/`.
  Panoply most likely descends from the second.
* *The standards tree.* The district map (`standards-map.toml`) is the right
  cure and is half executed: eight root directories exist only to hold a
  pointer, readiness grades are still at the root, and the reorg plan of
  2026-10-14 already sequences the rest. This plan does not repeat it; it adds
  one rule (<<target>>): a format's grammar, spec, tools, fixtures and mapping
  specs live under `1-formats/<format>/`, and nothing about that format lives
  elsewhere.

[[copilot]]
== The Copilot conversation, reviewed

=== What it proposes

An assurance envelope `AE = (Claims, Assumptions, Scope, Evidence, Boundaries,
Failure modes)`; an evidence-strength ladder E0–E5 mapped to numbers
0.1–1.0; reproducibility `Rp` as the mean of repeatability, independent
reproduction and context stability; validated learning `VL = ES × Rp × ΔB`;
"constitutional confidence" `CC = P(H|E) × Rp × ES`; exnovation rules E1–E4 on
posterior thresholds (0.70 / 0.40 / 0.20); a worked Bayesian example in which
AffineScript's theory falls from 0.90 to 0.09; and Panoply as a "discovery-driven
adaptive successor".

=== What it gets right, and where the estate already has it

[cols="2,3,3",options="header"]
|===
|Envelope part |Already in the estate |Gap

|Claims
|`ASSURANCE-CONSTITUTION.adoc` §Claims: every material claim labelled
`draft` / `designed` / `assumed` / `tested` / `audited` / `proven`
|No machine-readable claim record.

|Assumptions, scope, boundaries
|Same section: "subject, version, observation model, state boundary,
assumptions"; the verdict grid's scope footer; Panoply's "safety is scoped, not
global"
|Not typed; not checked.

|Evidence
|HYP-01 `support` / `evidence` / `level` (sound, epi, belief), machine-checked
in Hypatia; the deed spec's proposed *warrant* vs *receipt* vocabulary from
`epistemic-types`
|Not shared between Hypatia and the deed.

|Failure modes
|`bust` (active breakage); the constitution's reversibility classes
|*Genuinely new as a required field.* Worth adopting.

|Exnovation
|`dust` as planned retirement (coaptation reading); the amendment mechanism in
`CONTRACTILE-SPEC` (variance with expiry, amendment with ADR)
|No decision rule, no record of *why* something was retired or what would
reverse it.
|===

The envelope is therefore mostly a restatement of canon, plus two real
additions: *failure modes as a required field*, and *exnovation as a recorded
decision*.

=== What it gets wrong

*The history.* Panoply's README: "Panoply is a narrowing of one lesson from
AffineScript … It is not a rejection of AffineScript; AffineScript explores the
surface, Panoply disciplines the claim." AffineScript remains the estate's
primary application language in `.claude/CLAUDE.md`. The conversation's
"Exnovate(T_A)" is not a record of an estate decision and should not be cited
as one.

*The numbers.* The worked example's arithmetic is correct (re-run in
<<appendix-check>>): 0.90 → 0.871 → 0.628 → 0.090, and VL = 0.62.
But every likelihood was asserted, not measured, and the verdict depends on
them:

[cols="2,1,2",options="header"]
|===
|Likelihoods for E1–E3 |Final belief |Copilot band
|halved |0.012 |exnovation trigger
|as given |0.090 |exnovation trigger
|doubled |0.398 |review only
|===

*The formulas.* Four defects, each demonstrated:

. `CC = P(H|E) × Rp × ES` multiplies belief by evidence quality, so a
  hypothesis *strongly refuted* by excellent evidence (0.05 × 0.95 × 0.95 =
  0.045) and one with *no* evidence (0.5 × 0.1 × 0.1 = 0.005) both read as "low
  confidence". Belief and evidence are two axes; HYP-01 keeps them apart
  (`level` vs `support`), and so should any estate model.
. `Rp` as a mean lets a result nobody else has reproduced score 0.67. Independent
  reproduction is a *gate* for the stronger labels, not a term in an average.
. `VL` counts only belief *change*, so a strong, reproducible confirmation
  scores 0.07. Confirmations are learning too.
. Rule E1 exnovates on posterior alone. With the same posterior of 0.15,
  a cheap reversible switch should happen and a costly one should not; E1 says
  "exnovate" in both. Retirement is a decision under cost and reversibility,
  which the constitution already requires to be stated.

Finally, `DC = VL × CI` multiplies a number by an interval, which is undefined
as written.

=== What to keep (PROPOSED)

* *The envelope as a typed record.* One `(claims …)` clause in the chora deed,
  each claim carrying `:subject`, `:scope`, `:assumptions`, `:evidence` (paths
  or receipts), `:boundaries`, `:failure-modes`, and `:label` drawn from the
  constitution's six labels. Ordinal labels, not invented decimals.
* *A checkable rule.* Panoply's "no guarantee without an envelope; no envelope
  without evidence" is decidable over that record: a claim labelled `tested` or
  stronger with no evidence path, or `audited` with no independent reproduction,
  is a lint error. The template's `Coaptation.idr` already proves the same shape
  for `Must` coverage (`MayGap` has no constructor for `Must`), so the proof
  route exists.
* *Exnovation as a `dust` record.* A retirement carries basis, decision,
  switching cost, reversibility class and the evidence that would reverse it.
  `residual-evidence-types`' `Revision.agda` (already underwriting Hypatia's
  `Retraction.idr`) is the formal account of retraction to cite.
* *Drop* `CC`, `VL`, `DC` and the numeric ES table unless experiment X6 shows a
  calibrated version earns its place.

=== Fixed verb semantics

The rows below are the *recommended* resolution of the collisions; the
alternatives go to the owner (<<decisions>>).

[cols="1,3,1,1",options="header"]
|===
|Verb |Meaning |Authority |Grade

|`intend` |North star: committed intents with probes, and wishes. |reporting |tropical distance
|`must` |Invariants that block a release. |blocking |boolean
|`trust` |Security and provenance boundary. |blocking |boolean + provenance
|`adjust` |Accessibility and inclusive design: a gated floor plus an advisory posture. |advisory (floor may graduate to `must`) |graded [0,1]
|`dust` |Planned retirement (exnovation): what is being retired, why, the rollback path, the audit trail kept. |advisory |reported, never alarms
|`bust` |Active breakage and hard stops: what must not run, what has expired. |blocking |alarm
|===

Drift tolerance, the other reading of `adjust`, is a property of every verb (a
tolerance band on its grade), not a verb of its own. Cleanup of stale files, the
other reading of `dust`, is a `must` invariant ("no tracked build artefacts").

=== Naming

* *K9* means the component format and nothing else. The coordination file
  keeps its filename and is called "the coordination file" or
  "k9-coordination", never "a K9 file". The trust tiers are "leash levels"
  (Kennel, Yard, Hunt), as the contract already calls them.
* *Manifest* means `0-AI-MANIFEST.deed`. Per-directory ply manifests fold into
  the deed's `(ply …)` clause, as the template already did; per-verb manifests
  are gone.
* Every term above goes into `0-canon/CANONICAL-NAMES.adoc` as a homonym table,
  so the existing anti-normalisation directive covers it.

=== Versioning doctrine (PROPOSED)

One rule for all three formats, copied from the K9 contract:

. A format has one version, written in its ABNF header and its spec header, and
  they are equal. A spec document cannot be `DRAFT` while its grammar is
  normative and gated.
. An instance declares the *schema major.minor* it was written against
  (`:schema-version "1.2"` for a deed, `pedigree.schema_version` for K9,
  `metadata.schema_version` for coordination).
. A reader refuses another major and accepts any lower minor.
. Each format keeps a changelog in its spec, and a breaking change ships with a
  mapping spec and fixtures for the old major.
. `deed.abnf` v1.2.0 maps today's `:schema-version "1.0.0"` deeds to "1.0", which
  v1.2 accepts. No deed needs editing to adopt the rule.

== The Copilot model, recomputed

Run with `bun FILE.js`. Output is quoted in <<copilot>>.

[source,javascript]

const upd = (p, lh, lnh) => (lh * p) / (lh * p + lnh * (1 - p));
const steps = [[0.60, 0.80], [0.20, 0.80], [0.05, 0.85]];
let p = 0.90; const trace = [p];
for (const [a, b] of steps) { p = upd(p, a, b); trace.push(+p.toFixed(4)); }
console.log(trace);                               // [0.9, 0.871, 0.6279, 0.0903]
console.log((0.9 * 0.85 * Math.abs(0.9 - p)).toFixed(3)); // VL 0.619
const CC = (post, rp, es) => post * rp * es;
console.log(CC(.05, .95, .95), CC(.5, .1, .1));  // 0.045 vs 0.005
console.log((1 + 0 + 1) / 3);                     // Rp 0.67, no independent reproduction
for (const k of [0.5, 1, 2]) {
  let q = 0.9; for (const [a, b] of steps) q = upd(q, Math.min(1, a * k), b);
  console.log(k, q.toFixed(3));                   // 0.012, 0.090, 0.398
}
